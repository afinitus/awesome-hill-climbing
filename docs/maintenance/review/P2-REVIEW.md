# P2 catalog review — 2026-10-01

The required recent-paper cohort, reproducible older-paper sample, complete resource catalog, and every interrupted-pass entry lead received a primary-source description review. All 373 leads are processed; no lead remains unresolved. Verified corrections are applied to the source CSV files.

## Coverage and reproducibility

- **198 recent papers:** every paper dated on or after 2026-09-16. [Recent-paper manifests](P2-RECENT.csv) and [the remaining 50 rows](P2-RECENT-CHAPTER.csv) record the source and disposition. Metadata verification is separate from the mechanism/result checks.
- **117 older papers:** sampled from the 584 older, non-key, non-industry rows in their original CSV order. The draw uses `random.Random(20261001).shuffle(population)`, followed by the first `math.ceil(len(population)/5)` rows. The original population comes from the commit recorded in [P2-SELECTION.json](P2-SELECTION.json); the frozen IDs make the selection replayable after family corrections. The [sample manifest](P2-SAMPLE.csv) includes original and reviewed descriptions, exact changed fields, evidence and source hashes.
- **212 resources:** all current entries, including official page/repository identity, descriptions, claimed results and pertinent release/license details. The brief's count of 213 is stale. See [resource report](P2-RESOURCE-REVIEW.md) and its [212-row manifest](P2-RESOURCE-REVIEW.json).
- **373 interrupted-pass leads:** 307 paper leads and 66 resource leads were treated as allegations requiring fresh verification, not accepted evidence. Final dispositions, allegations, evidence and exact patches are recorded in [P2-LEADS.csv](P2-LEADS.csv). The completed union of the recent cohort, sample and paper leads comprises **559 distinct paper rows**; it does not mean 559 complete papers were read end to end.

Primary arXiv HTML or PDF, official repositories/project pages, publisher material and original company posts were used. Description-level checks covered mechanism, claimed result, baseline, evaluation setting and specific metadata/code-link issues. The review did not reproduce external experiments, exhaustively validate each paper's methodology, or independently establish authors' reported performance. Header/abstract checks were supplemented with the relevant methods, result tables, figures or release pages when needed. The repository-wide arXiv title/date and link checks are reported separately by the integration reviewer.

## Observed error rate

Before corrections, **63 of 117 sampled rows (53.8%)** had an issue warranting a change: **24 major (20.5%)** and **39 minor (33.3%)**. The remaining **54 (46.2%)** supported the checked claims. All identified sampled issues were corrected. **No sampled description check remained inaccessible or unresolved.** HTML failures were resolved through primary PDFs or official source pages; they were not counted as evidence of a false paper.

These are row-level rates: a row with several issues counts once, at its highest assigned severity. Major means a wrong attribution, incorrect result scope, mechanism/family error, or meaningful overclaim. Minor includes missing comparison/setting qualifiers, truncated affiliations, misleading code labels and editorial precision. Optional code additions and cosmetic preferences were not counted as errors. The Wilson 95% intervals are 44.8–62.6% for any issue and 14.2–28.7% for major issues; these describe sampling uncertainty, not a guarantee about remaining rows.

The random draw was fixed before inspecting the candidate issues. The deliberately suspicious candidate set is not a representative sample, so its correction rate must not be used to estimate error prevalence in untouched entries. Even the random sample estimates only this review's description-level findings, with reviewer judgment in severity classification. The major-issue rate is large enough that untouched rows should not be advertised as independently verified.

## Important corrections

The manifests preserve every reviewed row and exact patch; [P2-PATCHES.json](P2-PATCHES.json) records the before/after fields for changed rows and their evidence. Recurring substantive problems were comparisons against the wrong training stage, incomplete benchmark scope, unstated simulation or human-assistance assumptions, confusion between relative gains and percentage points, and descriptions of a baseline instead of the paper's contribution.

- **RAW-Dream, C292 — major:** the 43.4% comparator is a one-shot fine-tuned policy; “zero-shot” describes the world model's task coverage. Corrected the policy baseline instead of implying a zero-shot policy improved to 52.3%. [Primary paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.12334).
- **CIDER, C407 — major:** the 16.7% comparison belongs to the sixth stage rather than an average over stages. Corrected the comparison and described the verified perfect hardware result using its trial count, 100/100 per task. [Primary paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21899).
- **RISE, C174 — major:** binary-reward IDQL is a baseline, while the reported improvement uses a broader behavior policy trained with a spectral-norm penalty and DINOv2-neighbor action augmentation. Added the actual contribution and changed the family to offline/off-policy RL. [Primary paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.19495).
- **Zetta, N344 — major:** 90.8% covers the Goal settings rather than all reported LIBERO-Pro settings. The description now gives the 40 Goal/Long task-setting average, 32.0% to 71.1%, and its frozen-policy baseline. [Primary paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.16590).
- **RoboFAC, N348 — major:** the abstract's relative gains do not reproduce from the displayed tables. Used the table cells and named the metric and five-attempt budget: analysis score 79.10 versus 57.42, real success 61.25% versus 47.5% without correction. [Primary paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.12224).
- **QWM, C384 — major:** this trains the policy and critic online; the previous test-time-only family assignment was misleading. Removed an unbalanced weak-baseline plot comparison and described its verified predictive-search contribution. [Primary paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17163).
- **VLAPS / V-VLAPS, N198 / N088 — major:** the tree search uses the LIBERO simulator. Descriptions now expose that assumption; V-VLAPS also identifies its 600/1800-second episode search budgets and value-free baseline. [VLAPS](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.12211), [V-VLAPS](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.00969).
- **Release status — minor or major by claim:** checked the actual project and repository contents rather than equating an HTTP 200 with available code. Removed code labels from project-only or “Coming Soon” pages, including DoReMi, AIC MLLM, RoboTTT, R³ and VLA-Pilot, and from VLA-Reasoner's README/license-only placeholder. Replaced HAVE's project link with its verified implementation.
- **Resources:** 55 of 66 leads warranted a correction or clarification; 11 required no further change. Eight additional resource fixes bring the changed-row total to 63. The resource report distinguishes numerical/availability errors from attribution and wording improvements.

Several candidate allegations were rejected. Sword's primary text explicitly supports its within-world-model evaluation limitation; the RankQ entry already names the specific AntMaze comparison; incomplete but accurate short affiliations are not automatically false; and SRPO's candidate was an empty placeholder whose existing description was independently supported. This review did not accept an issue merely because it appeared in the interrupted audit.

## Access limits and remaining risk

All sampled claims were checkable. Elsewhere, some full-text access remained limited: the TechRxiv resource survey was narrowed to claims supported by its publisher-deposited Crossref abstract and authors' companion README; MDPI's direct page/PDF was blocked but indexed primary publisher text supported the retained description. These are evidence-limited source checks, not successful full-paper retrievals. N424's OpenReview access limitations and the conservative final wording are recorded in the lead disposition. Resource video entries were checked for identity/description, not watched in full.

DROID's official abstract/project says 84 tasks while its HTML abstract says 86; the catalog retains 84 with the supporting linked sources. Some papers also disagree between abstracts and result tables. Where a concrete table and setting supported a narrower description, that description replaced the ambiguous headline. No external result was silently represented as independently replicated.

All required P2 dispositions are recorded and all verified changes are applied. Pending the integrated checks, these fixes remove the identified catalog issues. The sample still gives a concrete reason for caution about untouched rows: this review supports a source-reported research catalog with a disclosed audit scope, not an error-free-corpus claim. The repository's final go/no-go also depends on the chapter and harness findings in the main report.
