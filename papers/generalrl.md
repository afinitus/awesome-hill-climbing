# Broader RL advances that transfer

[← Awesome Hill Climbing](../README.md) · [all families](README.md)

*Which general RL results from the last year change how you should hill-climb a robot policy?*

Not robot-manipulation papers: scaling laws for value functions and RL compute, critic and actor-critic design studies, flow/diffusion policy theory, RL for language models (group baselines, off-policy and stale-data training, process rewards, test-time compute), and last-mile RL for driving policies. Robot work keeps borrowing from all of them.

- **Loop step:** Background for every step
- **Built on:** Everything in Foundations
- **Use it when:** Read these when you are choosing an algorithm family or a compute budget, or when a robot paper cites an idea from language-model RL and you want the original.
- **What changed in 2025–26:** RL for language models and RL for robot policies increasingly share a toolkit in 2025-26: critic-free group baselines, KL-anchored fine-tuning from a pretrained model, learned verifiers, and spending compute at test time. Real-robot work still leans heavily on off-policy critics (SAC or TD3 with RLPD-style demo mixing), which language-model RL rarely uses.

## October 2026

- **[Probe the Harness](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02911)** (Pan, 2026-10-02) — Shows four harness details (ratio reference, unseeded arm, replayed first batch, loss normalisers) reversed a stale-data RL comparison between SAN and truncated importance sampling; once checked, TIS matches SAN on verl. Releases the PTH checklist.
- **[Towards Optimal Policy Improvement](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01566)** (Oren et al., 2026-10-01) — Frames greedification under approximate evaluation as decision-making under uncertainty and derives an operator optimal for that objective; it and its gradient-based approximations improve aggregate performance across GumbelAlphaZero, SAC, ReBRAC and Generalized Policy Iteration.

## September 2026

- **[AC2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39247)** (Wen et al., 2026-09-30) — AC2 assigns credit to 10k-token action chunks scored by a learned critic, used only where it is locally accurate, so trajectories need not reach a terminal reward; Qwen3-4B exceeds GRPO's 18.5% peak on IMO-ProofBench with 2.5x fewer decoding FLOPs.
- **[Cool the Sampler](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36953)** (Pan, 2026-09-29) — Sampling stale rollouts at temperature 0.8 while learner and importance weights stay at 1 stabilizes importance-corrected GRPO past its staleness cliff; Qwen2.5-Math-7B at refresh interval 144: cooled runs end at 92-93% vs 68-81% uncooled.
- **[PReFlow](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36812)** (Ha et al., 2026-09-29) — PReFlow extracts policies by critic-based selection of behavior proposals plus a conditional refinement flow trained with closed-form adjoint matching under a KL-regularized objective; highest aggregate OGBench score after online fine-tuning among compared methods, 91% after 500K steps.
- **[RoXDrive](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36851)** (CUHK-Shenzhen et al., 2026-09-29) — Closed-loop RL post-training of E2E driving policies inside video world models, keeping only rollouts an inverse-dynamics evaluator deems action-faithful; cuts safety violations 27.6% (DiffusionDrive, nuScenes). · [code](https://github.com/Hongbin98/RoXDrive)
- **[Fine-Tuning on Self-Generated and Reward-Weighted Data](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36945)** (Tsinghua; Alibaba, 2026-09-29) — Theory of RE(S), REINFORCE with rollouts refreshed every S steps, in softmax bandits: global convergence, Θ(1/T) rate, and moderate off-policyness escaping weak-initialization traps faster.
- **[EasyPPO](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36802)** (UC Berkeley; Princeton, 2026-09-29) — Stabilizes PPO's critic for LLM RL via actor-only truncation filtering, noise-normalized critic regression and smaller critic minibatches; relative gains of 14.89% coding, 2.28% math, 9.47% search over PPO. · [code](https://github.com/EasyPPO/EasyPPO)
- **[QAMM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.34497)** (Fudan; ShanghaiTech; Shanghai Innovation Institute, 2026-09-28) — Turns the critic's adjoint-matching signal into supervision for MeanFlow average velocity, yielding two-call flow policies competitive with flow-policy baselines on ten HumanoidMaze offline RL tasks.
- **[Learning from Teacher Continuations at Student States](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36246)** (UIUC; MIT; UPenn; University of Washington; IBM, 2026-09-28) — OLIVE: DAgger-like online LLM distillation; student generates prefix, teacher continues it, student trains by cross-entropy. +13% over offline SFT on ScienceWorld; async cuts training time 23.8%.
- **[RefineDrive](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.35078)** (Zhejiang University; Yinwang Intelligent Technology, 2026-09-28) — Simulator-state failure diagnosis retrieves corrections from a human trajectory bank; correction SFT precedes safety-reward GRPO. Driving VLA: NAVSIM v1 PDMS 87.7→91.7; the same checkpoint scores 89.4 EPDMS on original NAVTEST scenes under v2 metrics.
- **[Uni-TMPO](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.34688)** (HUST; CAS; SJTU; Frontis.AI; Tsinghua, 2026-09-28) — Turns group-standardized rewards into a target distribution and matches it by forward KL over trajectory log-probs instead of maximizing reward; for VLAs, on average +8.0/+4.8 points over the best reward-maximizing baselines on simulated task/scene OOD (MetaWorld/CALVIN).
- **[Safe Score Matching](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.33337)** (UC San Diego, 2026-09-27) — Off-policy actor-critic diffusion policy with a two-branch score target gated by an HJ reachability critic: Q-score matching when feasible, recovery denoising otherwise; tested on quadrotor, fixed-wing, Safety-Gymnasium. · [code](https://github.com/byli888/safe-score-matching)
- **[SAMBAR](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.32108)** (UC Berkeley, 2026-09-26) — Replay-free continual VLA fine-tuning as constrained optimization: method-of-multipliers dual penalty plus selective anchoring of parameters critical to earlier tasks; after three sequential LIBERO tasks, replay-free baselines score 0% on the first task, SAMBAR 84%.
- **[From Static Policies to Adaptive Priors in Offline Reinforcement Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.35880)** (Mila; Université de Montréal; McGill, 2026-09-26) — NeurIPS 2026 position paper: offline RL should produce adaptive policy priors that keep improving online via memory, exploration and self-correction (AORL); Bayesian offline RL as one route.
- **[Rank Collapse Is Recoverable, Growing \|Q\| Is Not: Out-of-Sample Early Warning for Value…](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.32819)** (Rutgers; Nanjing Tech; Nanjing University of Posts and Telecommunications; NYU, 2026-09-26) — In high-UTD SAC, rank collapse is survivable, but how far log\|Q\| has climbed by step 15k ranks runs by how soon the training guard flags them (out-of-sample AUC 0.78 Walker2d, 0.98 Ant, at UTD 4), enabling early aborts.
- **[Constrained Flow Policy Updates](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.32952)** (UC San Diego, 2026-09-26) — RAFALE: off-policy safe-RL actor-critic that differentiates an augmented objective through a flow policy's generation process, framed as a constrained Schrödinger bridge; cost within budget on seven Safety-Gymnasium tasks.
- **[MA-FPPO](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.32594)** (Sun Yat-sen University; NUS, 2026-09-26) — Online fine-tunes flow-matching-pretrained cooperative multi-agent policies via explicit action likelihoods and shared team advantages; average relative gains of 52.8% over the strongest offline baselines (30 settings) and 29.8% over purely online learning (38 matched-budget comparisons). · [code](https://github.com/ma-fppo/MA-FPPO)
- **[G2MAF](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31286)** (Sun Yat-sen University, 2026-09-25) — Refines frozen offline multi-agent flow-policy actions at test time with one globally normalized, projected critic gradient; improves 20 of 24 MPE/SMAC settings, with mean relative gains of 9.2% (MPE) and 8.9% (SMAC) at ~6% extra inference latency. · [code](https://github.com/g2maf/G2MAF)
- **[Hill Sampling for Test-Time Scaling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25510)** (Oracle, 2026-09-22) — Frozen-LLM hill climbing conditions new candidate programs on the best verified solution. In non-robotics tests, it outperforms repeated sampling and test-time ES; the paper reports circle-packing records relative to published methods. · entry corrected after source review
- **[DreamStream](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26792)** (UCLA; Toyota Research Institute, 2026-09-22) — Closed-loop driving simulator built on a simulator-grounded autoregressive video model; under its new FDpi fidelity metric it beats the strongest prior closed-loop simulator 1.6x on nuScenes and 4.7x on NAVSIM; its Navhard-CL benchmark exposes missing recovery behaviors. · [code](https://github.com/VAIL-UCLA/DreamStream)
- **[FAN](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21358)** (HKU; SUSTech; EIT Ningbo; HUST; INFIFORCE, 2026-09-18) — Compares five action-normalization strategies for continual VLA adaptation; FAN freezes stats from a small task-independent calibration set, avoiding coordinate drift, best across four real single-arm/bimanual task streams.
- **[CounterPlay](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21617)** (University of Freiburg; CARIAD; KIT, 2026-09-18) — Post-trains self-play driving policies by backtracking from failures via value estimates, retrying under reward-conditioned styles, keeping verified retries; BehaviorBench SOTA with 1B transitions (1% of anchor's 100B).
- **[REFINEPPO](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21108)** (Northeastern University, 2026-09-17) — PPO whose actor builds each action by iterative residual refinement: a shared network repeatedly corrects an initial proposal given state and current action. Matches or exceeds PPO on 14 Gymnasium control tasks, converging faster on several.
- **[EmbodiedMind](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19659)** (ZTE, 2026-09-17) — RL post-training of an embodied VLM (Qwen3-VL-8B) that outputs text plans and answers, not motor actions: rejection-sampling SFT, difficulty-queued IR-GRPO, then Trie-GRPO with prefix-tree step-level advantages. 70.02% average over 18 multimodal, spatial and planning benchmarks.
- **[OPTED](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20756)** (KE:SAI; ETH Zürich; NVIDIA; Stanford; ELLIS Tübingen, 2026-09-17) — RL-trained privileged teacher on vectorized inputs supervises closed-loop fine-tuning of camera driving policies in AlpaSim (3DGS); scene scores rise 1.6x (TransFuser) and 9.5x (VaVAM); in a separate vectorized WOMD study it matches RL fine-tuning with ~1000x fewer episodes.
- **[GR2PO](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19850)** (HKUST; HKUST Guangzhou, 2026-09-17) — Critic-free GRPO-style RL for continuous control: discounted returns from parallel trajectories, group-normalized per time index, clipped updates. MuJoCo Ant/Walker2d/Humanoid: beats immediate-reward GRPO, near PPO/SAC (trails PPO on Humanoid); Jetson TX2 test is inference-only.
- **[Rethinking Critic Learning in PPO](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18708)** (SJTU; Shanghai AI Lab; Westlake; Nanjing; Tsinghua; CUHK; NTU, 2026-09-16) — Identifies Value Flattening in PPO critics (flat predictions vs sharply varying Monte Carlo values); SP3O supervises only three well-separated states per response, improving LLM RL on Qwen3-Base.
- **[Not All Layers Need Tuning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18084)** (Carnegie Mellon University, 2026-09-16) — Shift diagnostics choose variable-rank LoRA adapters for VLA components; real xArm-7 instruction-shift trials yield 21/30 successes, compared with 22/30 after full fine-tuning, while updating only 0.04% as many parameters. · entry corrected after source review
- **[OptiFlow](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15883)** (Yonsei University, 2026-09-14) — Offline RL: state-wise entropic optimal transport couples a value-aware reference flow policy to a one-step policy, prioritizing high-value distillation targets without direct critic maximization; best state-based OGBench average (48.3 vs 45.6 for GFP). · [code](https://github.com/Yonsei-DILLab/OptiFlow)
- **[When Validation Stops Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10873)** (Rice University; UC Berkeley, 2026-09-09) — Audits policy-update admission: paired-binomial checks admit 31.6% of updates at 2,000 episodes/stage versus zero for a range-based gate in synthetic pushing. Unconditional replay learns better closed-loop; evidence remains analytical and simulated.
- **[DCRL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02237)** (Yonsei University; Seoul National University, 2026-09-02) — DCRL: recursively splits trajectory segments into a balanced binary tree, cutting worst-case bootstrap depth from linear to logarithmic; on the five hardest long-horizon OGBench tasks, raises the best prior average score from 55 to 64.

## August 2026

- **[Understanding Evolution Strategies for LLM Reasoning: Broader Reasoning Coverage than GRPO](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27351)** (Ba et al., 2026-08-27) — Analyzes ES post-training for LLM reasoning: unlike GRPO (entropy collapse), ES improves Pass@1 and reaches higher Pass@K than GRPO; gains concentrate in a sparse subset of larger-magnitude parameter updates; tests sequential GRPO/ES combinations (adjacent, LLMs).
- **[RoG-DAgger](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24525)** (Zhong et al., 2026-08-25) — DAgger uses short-horizon kinematic rollouts to generate driving corrections and time takeovers. In CARLA, improves SimLingo by 5.3 driving-score points on Bench2Drive and raises Fail2Drive OOD success from 55% to 66%.
- **[Le Critique](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.16739)** (Venkatraman et al., 2026-08-17) — Privileged value functions give critics information hidden from the policy; TETHER mixes value and group-relative baselines. Both improve over standard value baselines and are competitive with or outperform mean-baseline GRPO on tested reasoning tasks.
- **[FIRE-VLA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13395)** (Dou, 2026-08-13) — Low-reward, low-diversity groups trigger privileged-teacher self-distillation. Open-loop nuScenes, four-sample evaluation: mean L2 1.500m versus GRPO 1.848m (SFT 1.004m); single-sample improvement is inconclusive.
- **[Why Does Action Chunking Improve Behavioral Cloning Performance in Robotic Control?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02547)** (Lazzati et al., 2026-08-03) — Experiments attribute action-chunking gains to non-Markovian expressivity, reduced compounding error and implicit ensembling over delays. An explicit delayed-policy ensemble outperforms chunking in many tested settings.

## July 2026

- **[Deconstructing Actor-Critic](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.13274)** (Shah et al., 2026-07-14) — 33,000+ experiments on a simulated PID-gain-tuning task built from real water-treatment-plant data dissect actor-critic design choices; Gaussian policies with pathwise gradients are among the least reliable, bounded distributions with adaptive update schedules stay robust. · [code](https://github.com/haseebs/deconstruct-ac)
- **[Reward-Aware Population Scaling of Evolutionary Strategies in LLM Fine-Tuning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.19408)** (Cho et al., 2026-07-08) — In tested capable 0.5B–7B LLMs, z-score normalization can make binary-reward ES with N=2 fail; removing it improves GSM8K/TREC results. Derives zero-advantage probability and cautions that N=2 is not universally sufficient.

Residual-bootstrap RB-PEM improves COCO bbob-noisy, policy-search and hyperparameter-optimization results in tested noisy, fixed-budget settings. · [code](https://github.com/sichen-wang/Depth-over-Fidelity_ICML2026)

Image generation only. · [code](https://github.com/AndreasBergmeister/ram)

## February 2026

- **[LLMs Can Learn to Reason Via Off-Policy RL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.19362)** (Ritter et al., 2026-02-22) — OAPL: off-policy LLM post-training that embraces lagged inference policies (400+ gradient steps) instead of importance-sampling; beats GRPO+IS on competition math, matches DeepCoder with 3x fewer generations. · [code](https://github.com/danieldritter/OAPL)
- **[Stable Asynchrony](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.17616)** (Huang et al., 2026-02-19) — VCPO scales the learning rate by effective sample size and adds a critic-free minimum-variance baseline for stale-rollout policy gradients; stable even 128 steps off-policy, and matches synchronous performance 2.5x faster on a long-horizon tool-use task. · [code](https://github.com/mit-han-lab/vcpo)
- **[Maximum Likelihood Reinforcement Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.02710)** (CMU; ICML 2026, 2026-02-02) — Under binary terminal feedback, expected-reward RL approximates maximum likelihood to first order. MaxRL approaches likelihood optimization as sampling compute grows; math-reasoning evaluations report up to 20× test-time sampling efficiency over GRPO with a perfect verifier. · [code](https://github.com/tajwarfahim/maxrl) · entry corrected after source review

## January 2026

- **[Reinforcement Learning via Self-Distillation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.20802)** (Hübotter et al., 2026-01-28) — SDPO distills a feedback-conditioned LLM’s next-token predictions back into its policy. On nine selected very-hard LiveCodeBench questions, test-time self-distillation matches best-of-k discovery probability with about 3× fewer attempts. · [code](https://github.com/lasgroup/SDPO) · entry corrected after source review
- **[Self-Distillation Enables Continual Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.19897)** (Shenfeld et al., 2026-01-27) — SDFT: a demonstration-conditioned model acts as its own teacher for on-policy distillation, learning new skills from demos with less forgetting than supervised fine-tuning (foundation models, not robot-specific).

## December 2025

- **[Training Diffusion Policies via Prior-Mapping Co-Evolution](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.02581)** (Zhang et al., 2025-12-02) — GoRL: online RL for generative policies by optimizing a tractable latent prior, then consolidating into an expressive prior-to-action mapping; HopperStand return over 870, 3x strongest baseline (ICML 2026). · [code](https://github.com/bennidict23/GoRL)

## November 2025

- **[DIVO](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.08922)** (Ma et al., 2025-11-12) — DIVO (IROS 2025): trains a diffusion behavior model with binary advantage-based loss weights, then regularizes a TD3+BC actor toward diffusion samples with non-negative advantage; reports gains on D4RL locomotion and AntMaze.
- **[The Path Not Taken](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.08567)** (Zhu et al., 2025-11-11) — Three-Gate Theory for LLM RL with verifiable rewards: sparse-looking updates are a model-conditioned bias landing off principal weight directions with minimal spectral drift, unlike SFT.
- **[Learning Without Critics? Revisiting GRPO in Classical Reinforcement Learning Environments](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.03527)** (Oliveira et al., 2025-11-05) — Benchmarks critic-free GRPO against PPO on classical control: all critic-free baselines underperform PPO except on short-horizon tasks like CartPole; high discount factors mostly help; smaller group sizes outperform larger ones.
- **[RL without TD learning](https://bair.berkeley.edu/blog/2025/11/01/rl-without-td-learning/)** (UC Berkeley, 2025-11-01) — Transitive RL: divide-and-conquer off-policy value learning over in-dataset subgoals with expectile regression; matches best TD-n on OGBench long-horizon (up to 3,000-step) goal-conditioned tasks without tuning n.

## October 2025

- **[On-Policy Distillation](https://thinkingmachines.ai/blog/on-policy-distillation/)** (Thinking Machines Lab, 2025-10-27) — Student samples its trajectories; teacher supplies per-token reverse-KL feedback. Thinking Machines estimates 9–30x lower cost than extrapolated off-policy SFT for raising Qwen3-8B-Base from 60% to 70% on AIME 2024. · [code](https://github.com/thinking-machines-lab/tinker-cookbook/tree/main/tinker_cookbook/recipes/distillation)
- **[Transitive RL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.22512)** (Park et al., 2025-10-26) — Divide-and-conquer value update for offline goal-conditioned RL built on the triangle inequality; in principle needs O(log T) recursions versus TD's O(T), reducing bias accumulation; best results on long-horizon benchmarks. ICLR 2026. · [code](https://github.com/aoberai/trl)
- **[The Art of Scaling Reinforcement Learning Compute for LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.13786)** (Khatri et al., 2025-10-15) — 400k+ GPU-hour study fitting sigmoidal compute-performance curves to LLM RL: recipes differ in asymptote, while details like loss aggregation, normalization and curriculum mainly change compute efficiency; ScaleRL predictably extrapolates to a 100k GPU-hour run.
- **[A Survey of Process Reward Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.08049)** (Zheng et al., 2025-10-09) — Survey of step-level process reward models for LLMs: process data generation, PRM construction, use in test-time scaling and RL; applications span math, code, multimodal, robotics, agents.
- **[Group Policy Gradient](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.03679)** (Chen et al., 2025-10-04) — Critic-free policy gradient (GPG) replacing the value function with a group-based Monte Carlo advantage estimator inside PPO's clipped objective; matches or beats PPO and uses parallel simulation better.
- **[Prosperity before Collapse](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.01161)** (CMU; Meta AI, 2025-10-01) — M2PO constrains the second moment of importance weights for stable LLM RL on stale data. At staleness 256, clipped tokens average 0.06% versus GRPO’s 1.22%; experiments span six model scales. · [code](https://github.com/Infini-AI-Lab/M2PO)

## September 2025

- **[XQC](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.25174)** (Palenicek et al., 2025-09-29) — SAC actor-critic combines BatchNorm, WeightNorm and a distributional cross-entropy critic, reducing condition numbers by orders of magnitude; authors report strong sample efficiency across 55 proprioceptive and 15 vision tasks with fewer parameters. · [code](https://github.com/danielpalenicek/xqc) · entry corrected after source review

## Before September 2025

- **[Compute-Optimal Scaling for Value-Based Deep RL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.14881)** (Fu et al., 2025-08-20) — Studies compute allocation across model size, batch size and UTD ratio in online value-based RL; identifies TD-overfitting, where large batches hurt small critics but not large ones.
- **[IRL-VLA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.06571)** (Jiang et al., 2025-08-07) — Driving VLA: imitation pretraining, then PPO fine-tuning against a lightweight inverse-RL reward world model instead of a simulator; top NAVSIM v2 results, 2nd in CVPR2025 Autonomous Grand Challenge.
- **[Group Sequence Policy Optimization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.18071)** (Alibaba, 2025-07-24) — LLM RL algorithm replacing GRPO's token-level importance ratios with sequence-likelihood ratios and sequence-level clipping; more stable and efficient than GRPO, stabilizes MoE training, used for Qwen3.
- **[Action Chunking and Exploratory Data Collection Yield Exponential Improvements in…](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.09061)** (Zhang et al., 2025-07-11) — Control-theoretic analysis showing action chunking and exploratory (noise-injected) data collection each circumvent exponential compounding error in behavior cloning, in different regimes; validated on robot learning benchmarks.
- **[Horizon Reduction Makes RL Scalable](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.04168)** (Park et al., 2025-06-04) — Many offline RL methods saturate despite datasets up to 1,000x larger than typical. Horizon-reduction experiments improve scaling on challenging tasks; SHARSA achieves the best asymptotic performance among the evaluated methods. · [code](https://github.com/seohongpark/horizon-reduction)
- **[ProRL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.24864)** (NVIDIA, 2025-05-30) — Prolonged RL adds KL control, reference-policy resets and diverse tasks to DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-1.5B. Pass@k gains are largest where the base is weak; some high-baseline math tasks show unchanged or lower pass@128. · [code](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/Nemotron-Research-Reasoning-Qwen-1.5B)
- **[The Entropy Mechanism of Reinforcement Learning for Reasoning Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.22617)** (Cui et al., 2025-05-28) — Shows LLM policy-gradient RL trades policy entropy for performance, driven by probability-logit covariance; Clip-Cov and KL-Cov restrict high-covariance token updates to prevent entropy collapse.
- **[Reinforcement Learning Finetunes Small Subnetworks in Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.11711)** (Mukherjee et al., 2025-05-16) — Across 7 RL algorithms (PPO, GRPO, DPO...) and 10 LLMs, RL fine-tuning updates only 5-30% of parameters; these sparse updates are nearly full-rank. NeurIPS 2025.
- **[Does Reinforcement Learning Really Incentivize Reasoning Capacity in LLMs Beyond the…](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.13837)** (Tsinghua University; Shanghai Jiao Tong University, 2025-04-18) — RLVR-trained LLMs win at pass@1 but base models win at large-k pass@k: RL improves sampling efficiency within base-model capability, unlike distillation; NeurIPS 2025 Oral. · [code](https://github.com/LeapLabTHU/limit-of-RLVR)
- **[Understanding R1-Zero-Like Training](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.20783)** (Sea AI Lab, 2025-03-26) — Dr. GRPO removes a response-length optimization bias, improving token efficiency while maintaining reasoning performance. The paper’s complete minimalist R1-Zero recipe reaches 43.3% AIME 2024 accuracy with a 7B base model. · [code](https://github.com/sail-sg/understand-r1-zero)
- **[1000 Layer Networks for Self-Supervised RL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.14858)** (Wang et al., 2025-03-19) — Scaling contrastive goal-conditioned RL from 4 to 64 layers gives 2–50x gains on simulated tasks without demonstrations or rewards; Humanoid U-Maze improves further up to 1,024 layers. · [code](https://github.com/wang-kevin3290/scaling-crl)
- **[DAPO](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.14476)** (Yu et al., 2025-03-18) — Decoupled Clip and Dynamic Sampling Policy Optimization: four techniques for large-scale LLM RL; 50 points on AIME 2024 with Qwen2.5-32B; code on verl plus dataset released. · [code](https://github.com/BytedTsinghua-SIA/DAPO)
- **[So You Think You Can Scale Up Autonomous Robot Data Collection?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.01813)** (Mirchandani et al., 2024-11-04) — CoRL 2024 study over 7 sim and real tasks: autonomous imitation-learning data collection inherits RL's environment-design burdens; scaling human demos often improves policies significantly more.
- **[The Promise of Generalist Robotic Policies](https://sergeylevine.substack.com/p/the-promise-of-generalist-robotic)** (Sergey Levine, 2024-10-06) — Essay: generalist robot foundation models bootstrap a deployment data flywheel; language feedback and RL from real-world outcomes then drive autonomous self-improvement beyond imitation.
- **[Stop Regressing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.03950)** (Google DeepMind, 2024-03-06) — Trains value functions with categorical cross-entropy instead of MSE regression, improving performance and scalability on Atari, Q-transformer robot manipulation, chess and Wordle by mitigating noisy, non-stationary targets.
- **[Fine-tuning Reinforcement Learning Models is Secretly a Forgetting Mitigation Problem](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.02868)** (Wołczyk et al., 2024-02-05) — RL fine-tuning can forget pretrained capabilities on states not visited early; knowledge-retention methods mitigate this. NetHack Human Monk exceeds 10K points versus the previous neural-model best of 5K.
- **[Guided Flows for Generative Modeling and Decision Making](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.13443)** (Meta, UCLA, Weizmann Institute, 2023-11-22) — Adds classifier-free guidance to flow matching. Improves image and speech generation, and for offline-RL planning runs about 10x faster than diffusion with comparable returns.
- **[Self-Improving Robots and the Importance of Data](https://sergeylevine.substack.com/p/self-improving-robots-and-the-importance)** (Sergey Levine, 2022-08-22) — Levine essay: a Roomba-scale fleet (~10 million service robots) would collect ~10 billion hours of embodied experience a year; offline RL on self-supervised, goal-conditioned tasks is proposed as the way to learn from it.
- **[Reverse Curriculum Generation for Reinforcement Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/1707.05300)** (UC Berkeley, 2017-07-17) — Starts from one goal state and adaptively expands training starts outward, forming a reverse curriculum for sparse-reward simulated navigation and fine-grained manipulation (CoRL 2017).
