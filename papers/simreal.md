# Sim-to-real & real-to-sim

[← Awesome Hill Climbing](../README.md) · [all families](README.md)

*How do I get the trial-and-error gains of RL for a robot policy without paying for thousands of risky, hand-reset real-world episodes?*

Uses a simulator, often a scanned or generated copy of the real scene, as a safe practice gym where the policy can hill-climb with RL before or alongside a short real-robot stage. Real demos or real replay data keep it tied to reality.

- **Loop step:** Deploy → Improve
- **Built on:** Domain randomization; Real-to-sim: digital twins and cousins; BC-anchored sim RL (sim-real co-training); Sim-to-online RL on the real robot; Sim value as a shaping signal; Residual correction on a sim-made base; Gradient-free and label-light last-mile knobs for frozen policies
- **Use it when:** Use this family when real-world trial and error is slow, unsafe or needs human resets, and you can build a simulator that gets the coarse task structure right: rigid objects, known geometry, a calibratable controller. With a pretrained BC or VLA policy, a few real demos and a rough simulator, run sim RL with a real-data BC anchor (RL-Co, TacCoRL, DLS); for recurring deployment failures, rebuild the failing scenes in simulation (F4R); for a small actor-critic you can run on hardware for minutes, pretrain in sim and continue online. Avoid it for deformables, fluids or contact physics your simulator cannot fake, or when building the twin costs more than collecting real data.
- **What changed in 2025–26:** The simulator changed jobs: earlier sim-to-real trained small policies from scratch and hoped for zero-shot transfer, while now it is also a practice gym for already-pretrained BC and VLA policies, often with real demonstrations kept as a BC anchor during sim RL (RL-Co, Feb 2026; TacCoRL; DLS). Building the simulation got cheaper and more automatic, from phone-scanned twins (TwinRL) to generated 3D scenes (Generative 3D Worlds, Mar 2026), which makes it possible to rebuild only the scenes where the real policy failed (F4R, Sep 2026). Sim-real hybrids also matured: TwinRL runs RL in the twin to seed the real replay buffer, and What Matters for Sim-to-Online RL sets out how to continue sim-pretrained SAC on a real robot.

## September 2026

- **[Track-and-Complete](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36924)** (Yonsei University, 2026-09-29) — TRACC imitates the usable motion prefix of a failed human video, then trains with a VLM-proposed task-completion reward to reach the intended goal; evaluated in simulation (Unitree G1, Isaac Gym) on six Oops! failures. · [code](https://tracc-humanoid.github.io)
- **[Counterfactual Video Generation Enables Scalable Humanoid Loco-Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38172)** (UC Berkeley; Stanford; Amazon FAR, 2026-09-29) — PRISM expands a few human videos into hundreds of counterfactual interactions, reconstructs physically plausible simulation trajectories, and trains depth-based humanoid policies for joystick-controlled picking, carrying and dropping, transferred zero-shot to real hardware. · [code](https://prism-real2sim2real.github.io/)
- **[Real2Gym](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37089)** (Ren et al., 2026-09-29) — Agentic Real2Sim2Real: reconstructs simulation gyms from videos; an agent writes stage-level manipulation code and distills successes and failures into reusable skills without weight updates; zero-shot real Franka 9/12 trials vs 5/12 for GPT-6 Astra Direct Mode. · [code](https://real2gym.github.io/)
- **[DexAgent](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.35318)** (Stanford University; Columbia, 2026-09-28) — Agentic pipeline with property-specific verifiers and a self-evolving tool library turns a single egocentric human video into sim-generated robot trajectories for training pi0.5; 63.6% average success over 11 real dexterous tasks, about 3.5x SPIDER's. · [code](https://dexagent123.github.io/)
- ⭐ **[F4R](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.35575)** (Nanyang Technological University; Xi'an Jiaotong University; Dexmal, 2026-09-28) — A VLM diagnoses real pi0.5 failures, the scene is rebuilt in Isaac Sim, and the policy is co-trained on failure-conditioned sim data, then PPO-refined and redeployed. Real OOD success: 26.25% → 90.0%, vs 71.25% for budget-matched Targeted BC.
- **[DexTaG](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.33882)** (Yang et al., 2026-09-27) — Uses glove tactile signals as RL guidance to steer policy search toward human contact patterns when retargeting mocap to robot hands; learns marker/hammer use, beats distance-based rewards.
- **[ZeroBot](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.34010)** (Kapelyukh et al., 2026-09-27) — Single-view image-to-3D meshes feed massively parallel sim RL with a contact-state-sampling action space; no demos or pretraining, 87% real-world success after ~119 s training. · [code](https://zerobot-rl.github.io)
- **[Online Sim-to-Real Adaptation via Closed-Loop System Modeling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28878)** (Huang et al., 2026-09-24) — OSRAM: meta-trains a closed-loop dynamics model in randomized sim, fine-tunes on limited real data, then optimizes reference commands online for a frozen controller; reduces bipedal tracking error on hardware. · [code](http://generalroboticslab.com/OSRAM)
- **[Morphometric Imitation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28660)** (Sadjadpour et al., 2026-09-23) — Contact-aware morphometric retargeting of human hand motion, RL refinement for dynamic feasibility, then visuomotor distillation; 89.3% zero-shot success over 300 real trials on 30 objects. · [code](https://morphometricimitation.github.io)
- **[Visuomotor Robotic Pruning in Planar Orchards Using Hybrid Reinforcement Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24906)** (Oregon State University, 2026-09-21) — Hybrid PPO mixing offline motion-planner demos with online sim rollouts trains an optical-flow visuomotor pruning policy; 49.9%/46.0% sim success (apples/cherries), zero-shot transfer in 38 physical trials, and 7/15 vs 4/15 for point-cloud RRT-Connect in the lab.
- **[InsertAnything](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24511)** (Ma et al., 2026-09-21) — Sim-only RL insertion policy using 3D fingertip force feedback and a decoupled gated reward; real insertion down to 0.02 mm nominal clearance, 20/20 on ManipulationNet peg-in-hole (Human-in-the-Loop protocol, autonomous insertion motions), and 95.0% (152/160) zero-shot on eight unseen real tasks. · [code](https://mzhsoul.github.io/InsertAnything/)
- **[A Sim-to-Real Integration Pipeline for Training and Deployment of Chunk-Based VLA Manipulation Policies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21817)** (Kappel et al., 2026-09-18) — Replays simulated expert trajectories open-loop on a real Franka FR3 to record real observations, unifying data collection, closed-loop evaluation and sim-to-real gap measurement for chunk-based VLAs. · [code](https://gitlab.isir.upmc.fr/kappel/sim2real_public_chunk_control)
- **[Accelerating Visual Policy Learning with Sampling-Based Model Predictive Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20575)** (Yale University; University of Sydney, 2026-09-17) — SGPS: BC-initialized depth policies alternate sampling-based MPC action-target refinement with short-horizon first-order policy-gradient updates in differentiable simulation; learns simulated Go2/G1 skills, and Go2 specialists are distilled into one depth policy that transfers zero-shot to a real Go2.
- **[RebarSim](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20477)** (Sun et al., 2026-09-17) — RebarSim: privileged state-based RL teacher on procedural rebar geometries, DAgger-distilled into a multi-view RGB student; zero-shot 91.3% (137/150) real seating at 1.4 mm clearance; adapting to a new design takes 4-6x fewer distillation samples than training from scratch. · [code](https://rebarsim.github.io)
- **[DEXTERA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21045)** (UT Austin, University of Florida, BrainCo, 2026-09-17) — Single-image real-to-sim-to-real pipeline for dexterous hands (Gaussian background, VLM-inferred physics, VR teleop, trajectory synthesis); on six task pairs, 1:1 sim-real co-training lifts mean real success of ACT, DP and pi0.5 from 29.2% (sim-only) to 61.9%.
- **[Function-Preserving Data Generation for Zero-Shot Real-to-Sim-to-Real Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18293)** (Xiang et al., 2026-09-16) — Generates synthetic demos from reconstructed assets without teleoperated source trajectories, using constraint-guided mesh deformation that preserves contact geometry; zero-shot deployment on contact-rich long-horizon tasks, no real fine-tuning. · [code](https://fpsa-r2s2r.github.io/)
- ⭐ **[DLS (Where Success Breaks)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06114)** (Show Lab, National University of Singapore, 2026-09-05) — Finds pi0/pi0.5 failures in parallel digital twins, turns hand-defined task phases into a dense potential, and applies a critic-free contrastive velocity loss plus a real-demo SFT anchor. pi0.5, 3 real tasks, ID/Unseen: 84.4/72.2 vs PPO 81.1/67.8, 100-demo SFT 78.9/54.4.

## August 2026

- **[DREAM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29078)** (Sato et al., 2026-08-29) — Reconstructs the deployment workspace in sim, translates instructions to symbolic goals via LLM, and uses TAMP to generate verified, randomized demos for VLA fine-tuning without human teleoperation.
- **[SCAPE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19425)** (Zhu et al., 2026-08-19) — Predicts scenario-level real-world policy performance from few paired sim/real samples plus many sim rollouts, with sim-bias correction and conformal calibration. Sim-to-sim: error down 4.9%/34.7% (driving), 14.5%/27.7% (quadruped) vs neural/aggregate baselines; also run on a real Go2.

## July 2026

- **[RoboSnap](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.06699)** (Zhang et al., 2026-07-07) — Single RGB image to sim-ready scene (collision-aware foreground assets, 3DGS background); releases DROID-Sim from 564 real scenes for replay, synthetic data generation and sim-real correlated policy evaluation. · [code](https://github.com/robosnap/robosnap)
- ⭐ **[SILO](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.04616)** (NVIDIA; UC San Diego, 2026-07-06) — Trains PPO from scratch in sim on a capsule-chain cable for single-clip routing, then deploys through a 10 Hz twin whose sim joint angles become real targets. Real 3-clip routing, zero demos: 18/24 vs 12/24 copied from the hierarchical-IL paper.

## June 2026

- **[SimFoundry](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.28276)** (Ranawaka et al., 2026-06-26) — Reconstructs video-to-sim digital twins and affordance-preserving object, scene and task variants for policy training. Across seven manipulation tasks and five policies, simulated evaluations correlate with real performance (mean Pearson 0.911). · [code](https://github.com/NVlabs/SimFoundry)
- **[Play2Perfect](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.26428)** (Lum et al., 2026-06-24) — Two-stage RL for multi-fingered hands: task-agnostic play pretraining on diverse objects, then sparse-reward assembly fine-tuning; 33x more sample-efficient than RL from scratch (even with dense rewards); zero-shot real 60% (6/10) on 0.5 mm-clearance insertions. · [code](https://github.com/kushal2000/play2perfect)
- ⭐ **[TacCoRL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.11743)** (UCLA, UC San Diego, Peking University, University of Utah, UESTC, 2026-06-10) — Adds contact-gated tactile tokens to a pi0.5-style VLA, co-trains on real plus sim demos, then runs sim PPO with a real-data BC anchor. Real, 4 bimanual tasks after sim RL: 72.5% visuo-tactile vs 50.0% vision-only.
- ⭐ **[Video2Sim2Real](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.08828)** (Georgia Institute of Technology, University of Pennsylvania, Toyota Research Institute, Carnegie Mellon University, 2026-06-07) — Builds a sim twin from one RGB-D human video, re-optimizes robot poses at object-motion keyframes, distills them into a point-cloud policy, adds a PPO finger residual. Real, 7 tasks, no robot demos: 67/70 (95.7%) vs 11/70 with FoundationPose poses. · [code](https://github.com/video2sim2real/video2sim2real)

## March 2026

- ⭐ **[Sim-to-Real VLA Generalization Study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.22876)** (The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen; Shenzhen Loop Area Institute, 2026-03-24) — Controlled study of zero-shot sim-to-real for a VLA trained only in RoboTwin 2.0, isolating which randomization factors matter and adding binary-success GRPO in sim. Real average success: SFT 5.6% → SFT+RL 33.4% → SFT+RL+DR 42.8%.
- ⭐ **[Sim-to-Real VLA RL with Generative 3D Worlds](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.18532)** (Horizon Robotics, 2026-03-19) — Generates 100 physical ManiSkill 3 scenes with a GPT-4o designer and EmbodiedGen, runs PPOFlow on a BridgeV2-pretrained pi0 across them, then transfers zero-shot. Real WidowX, 12 scenes, 240 trials: 21.7% → 75% success, compared only with the imitation starting policy.
- **[OmniReset](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.15789)** (University of Washington; Microsoft Research, 2026-03-16) — OmniReset: diverse programmatic simulator resets let large-scale RL learn long-horizon dexterous manipulation without hand-crafted curricula or demos; scales with compute and transfers to real robots above baselines. · [code](https://github.com/UW-Lab/UWLab)
- ⭐ **[HandelBot](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.12243)** (Stanford University; Amazon FAR, 2026-03-12) — Freezes a sim piano rollout, corrects lateral finger joints from MIDI errors, then trains bounded residual RL with MIDI rewards. Five real songs: mean best-validation key-press F1 77.6 versus open-loop simulation trajectory 41.8 (chart reading). · [code](https://github.com/amberxie88/handelbot)

## February 2026

- ⭐ **[What Matters for Sim-to-Online RL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.20220)** (ETH Zurich; Google DeepMind, 2026-02-23) — Empirical study of continuing sim-pretrained SAC on real robots: keep or warm-start replay, update the actor once per 20 critic steps at LR 1e-5, restore optimizer state. Franka vision grasping reaches near-perfect success in about ten real minutes. · [code](https://github.com/yardenas/panda-rl-kit)
- ⭐ **[RL-Co (RLinf-Co / Beyond Imitation)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.12628)** (Tsinghua University, Harbin Institute of Technology, Peking University, Carnegie Mellon University, Shanghai AI Laboratory, Zhongguancun Academy, 2026-02-13) — Co-trains the VLA on real plus MimicGen sim demos, then runs PPO in a rough ManiSkill3 task copy with a weighted real-demo BC loss; no real-robot RL. Real average success, pi0.5: SFT co-training 45.9 → 66.2. · [code](https://github.com/RLinf/RLinf)
- ⭐ **[TwinRL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.09023)** (Peking University; HKUST; Simplexity Robotics; Tsinghua University, 2026-02-09) — SFTs Octo-Small on 30 real demos plus twin trajectories, runs RL in a phone-scanned twin to seed the real buffer, then real HIL-SERL-style RL. 4 real Franka tasks: near-100% success, at least 30% less real interaction than HIL-SERL. · [code](https://github.com/zhourui9813/TwinRL)

## October 2025

- ⭐ **[VT-Refine](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.14930)** (Columbia University, NVIDIA, UC San Diego, 2025-10-16) — BC-trains a visuo-tactile diffusion policy on 30 real demos per task, fine-tunes it with DPPO and sparse success reward in an Isaac Gym twin simulating the same tactile pad, and redeploys. Real tabletop, 5 parts, visuo-tactile mean: 0.50 → 0.85. · [code](https://github.com/NVlabs/vt-refine)
- **[Online Imitation-Pretrained World Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.02538)** (UC San Diego, UC Berkeley, UNC Chapel Hill, Sudo AI, 2025-10-02) — Reward-free online imitation RL pretrains a TD-MPC2-style world model in sim for coverage, then a 15-30 demo real fine-tune. Real 32/40 vs 15/40 for the best baseline (v2 numbers; v1 differed).

## September 2025

- **[Generalizable Domain Adaptation for Sim-and-Real Policy Co-Training](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.18631)** (Cheng et al., 2025-09-23) — Unbalanced optimal-transport loss aligns joint observation-action features across abundant sim and scarce real demos during BC co-training; up to ~30% real-world success gain (NeurIPS 2025). · [code](https://github.com/GaTech-RL2/ot-sim2real)

## Before September 2025

- **[X-Sim](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.07096)** (Cornell, 2025-05-11) — Reconstructs a photorealistic sim from an RGBD human video, trains RL on object-centric rewards, distills into an image diffusion policy with online sim-real alignment; no teleoperation data. Average task progress +30% over hand-tracking and sim-to-real baselines on 5 real tasks. · [code](https://github.com/portal-cornell/X-Sim)
- **[Sim-and-Real Co-Training](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.24361)** (UT Austin, NVIDIA, UC Berkeley, New York University, 2025-03-31) — Co-trains one Diffusion Policy on 20-50 real demos per task plus MimicGen-multiplied sim 'digital cousins' and prior sim data. Across six real tasks, average success rose from 45.3% (real only) to 83.2% (real + cousins + prior).
- **[Re3Sim](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.08645)** (Han et al., 2025-02-12) — Real-to-sim pipeline using 3D reconstruction and neural rendering inside a physics simulator; imitation policies trained only on its sim data exceed 58% average zero-shot real success. · [code](https://github.com/InternRobotics/Re3Sim)
- **[SGFT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.02705)** (University of Washington, Microsoft Research, 2025-02-04) — Precursor. The frozen sim critic serves as a potential-based shaping reward and H-step terminal value for real-world SAC/TD-MPC2. 100% on hammering and pushing within about an hour, 70% on insertion within two hours. · [code](https://github.com/WEIRDLabUW/sgft)
- **[CASHER](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.01770)** (Torne et al., 2024-12-02) — Crowdsourced real-to-sim scans plus sim RL bootstrapped by human demos; the improving generalist policy replaces human effort, giving zero/few-shot real transfer and scan-only fine-tuning.
- **[ACDC (Digital Cousins)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.07408)** (Stanford University, 2024-10-09) — Precursor. Sim 'cousin' scenes built from one image. Zero-shot real door opening: 25% for the exact twin vs 90% cousin-only and 95% twin+cousin. · [code](https://github.com/cremebrule/digital-cousins)
- **[RL-GSBridge](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.20291)** (Shanghai Jiao Tong University and others, 2024-09-30) — Precursor. A 3DGS-rendered twin lets image-based SAC transfer zero-shot. small_cube grasping 96.88% sim → 96.88% real, vs 12.50% real for mesh renders. · [code](https://github.com/IRMV-Manipulation-Group/RL-GSBridge)
- **[SplatSim](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.10161)** (Carnegie Mellon University, 2024-09-16) — Precursor. Gaussian-splat rendering over PyBullet physics. A zero-shot RGB diffusion policy averages 86.25% sim-to-real vs 97.5% real-to-real. · [code](https://github.com/qureshinomaan/SplatSim)
- **[DemoStart](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.06613)** (Google DeepMind, 2024-09-10) — Uses a few sim demos and a sparse reward to build an auto-curriculum for RL on a three-fingered hand, then distills and transfers zero-shot to real. Needs 100x fewer demos than real-robot baselines.
- **[RialTo](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.03949)** (MIT, University of Washington, TU Darmstadt, 2024-03-06) — Precursor. Phone-scan twin, then PPO with a BC term starting from 15 demos, then distill back. Real success averages 91%/77%/75% (poses/distractors/disturbances) vs 25%/11%/5% for BC. · [code](https://github.com/real-to-sim-to-real/RialToPolicyLearning)
- **[RMA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.04034)** (Kumar et al., 2021-07-08) — Sim-trained base policy plus adaptation module lets an A1 quadruped adapt to new terrains in fractions of a second, deployed without real-world fine-tuning (RSS 2021). · [code](https://ashish-kmr.github.io/rma-legged-robots/)
