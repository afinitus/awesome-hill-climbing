# Residual, edit & steering policies

[← Awesome Hill Climbing](../README.md) · [all families](README.md)

*How do I push a big imitation, diffusion or VLA policy from 50% to 95%+ when I can't cheaply fine-tune or backprop through it, without breaking what it already does well?*

Freeze the big pretrained policy and train a small add-on with RL: one that corrects its actions (residual/edit) or one that picks the noise it is fed (steering).

- **Loop step:** Improve
- **Built on:** Residual policy: the base is part of the environment; Start at base performance: zero-init, bound and scale the correction; Off-policy actor-critic with prior data is what makes this fit on a real robot; Latent-noise steering: RL picks the noise, not the action; Edit policy + best-of-N selection by Q; Support versus reach
- **Use it when:** Use it when you already have a decent base (roughly 20-80% success) that you can't or won't fine-tune: a VLA too big to backprop through with RL, a closed checkpoint that allows only forward calls, or a chunked diffusion or ACT policy whose likelihood is awkward for policy gradients. It also fits when you want the base's generality and recovery behaviors kept intact, and when your budget is minutes to hours of real robot time with sparse success labels.
- **What changed in 2025–26:** Over the last 12 months, bases moved from task-specific diffusion or ACT policies to frozen generalist VLAs (pi0, pi0.5, GR00T, SmolVLA, OpenVLA-OFT). Freezing is common because RL through a multi-billion-parameter model is costly, and an untouched base keeps behaviors the new task data never exercise, such as recovery from failure (the case PPS makes for steering a frozen base). Real-robot add-ons are usually trained off-policy (ResFiT, DICE-RL, EXPO-FT, MiDAS); ResFiT reports about 200x the sample efficiency of a PPO residual in simulation. The limits of each branch became clearer: noise steering stays inside the base's support (on one-demo LIBERO-Long, DSRL stays at the BC start's 33.5 while MiDAS's residual actor reaches 91.2), and RFS pairs noise steering for global exploration with a residual for local correction. The EXPO line pairs a large proposer with a small editor and picks the argmax-Q action: Real-Time EXPO-FT reports 42% -> 97% on four dynamic real-world tasks with online data capped at 10 minutes.

## Before September 2025

- **[DSRL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.15799)** (UC Berkeley; U Washington; Amazon, 2025-06-18) — RL over the input noise of a frozen diffusion or flow policy. Real Franka 2/10 → 9/10 after about 40 episodes. Real pi0 'turn on toaster' 5/20 → 18/20 after 80 episodes. · [code](https://github.com/ajwagen/dsrl)
- **[Policy Decorator](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.13630)** (UC San Diego, 2024-12-18) — Bounded SAC residual plus a progressive exploration schedule on frozen BeT or Diffusion Policy. Bases at 13-78% reach near-perfect success on 8 sim tasks, with millions of env steps. · [code](https://github.com/tongzhoumu/policy_decorator) · entry corrected after source review
- **[ResiP](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.16677)** (MIT Improbable AI; Harvard, 2024-07-23) — Per-step PPO residual on a frozen chunked diffusion policy. Sim assembly goes from 5-54% to 88-99% (low randomization) with 50 demos, while diffusion BC plateaus at about 80% on one_leg even with 100K RL-generated demos. · [code](https://github.com/ankile/robust-rearrangement)
- **[Residual-MPPI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.00898)** (UC Berkeley; Sony Research; UT Austin, 2024-07-01) — Online MPPI planning adapts a pretrained policy to new objectives using only its action distribution, no retraining; customizes champion GT Sophy racing agent few-shot and zero-shot. · [code](https://github.com/outsider86/Residual-MPPI)
- **[TRANSIC](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.10315)** (Stanford, 2024-05-16) — Gated residual trained by max likelihood on human SpaceMouse corrections over a frozen sim policy. Real Franka average 81% over four tasks; beats BC fine-tuning, and HG-DAgger and IWR, which the authors find forget the base policy. · [code](https://github.com/transic-robot/transic)
- **[RESPRECT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.14858)** (Ceola et al., 2024-01-26) — Learns a residual policy on top of a DRL-pretrained multi-fingered grasping policy, reusing its components; about 5x fewer timesteps than from scratch, validated on the real iCub.
- **[PLAS](https://arxiv.org/abs/2011.07213)** (CMU, 2020-11-14) — Offline RL whose actor outputs a tanh-bounded latent through a frozen CVAE behavior decoder, constraining actions through its learned data distribution; evaluated on simulated continuous control and real deformable-object manipulation. · [code](https://github.com/Wenxuan-Zhou/PLAS)
- **[Residual Policy Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/1812.06298)** (MIT, 2018-12-15) — pi = pi_base + f_theta with DDPG+HER on a frozen hand controller or MPC. On sim Push it reaches 0.9 success with about 10x fewer samples than learning from scratch. · [code](https://github.com/k-r-allen/residual-policy-learning)
- **[Residual RL for Robot Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/1812.03201)** (Siemens; UC Berkeley; TU Hamburg, 2018-12-07) — TD3 residual on a hand-engineered controller. Real Sawyer insertion with misaligned blocks: 15/20 vs 2/20 for the controller, in about 8K samples (about 3 h).
